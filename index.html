04 / MARKET DISCIPLINE

Three filters before the word arbitrage.

A mathematical mismatch is not automatically a trade. The Observatory keeps measurement quality visible instead of converting every discrepancy into a false opportunity.

01 · SYNCHRONIZATION

Quotes or reference observations must be close enough in time for a meaningful comparison. Otherwise the result is classified as non-synchronous.

02 · FRICTION

Bid/ask spread, fees and other known costs must be considered before a residual gap can be described as potentially executable.

03 · PERSISTENCE

One observation can be noise. Repeated gaps are tracked separately so structural fragmentation can be distinguished from a transient print.

BondStats Global Arbitrage Observatory is an analytical research tool, not a trading recommendation. Reference-rate comparisons can differ because of publication time, market convention, liquidity and methodology. A displayed dislocation should not be interpreted as risk-free profit.
